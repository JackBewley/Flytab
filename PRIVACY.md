# Flytab Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Flytab switches between recently used open Chrome tabs across browser windows. The extension processes tab information on your device and does not transmit it to the publisher or third parties.

## Information used by the extension

Flytab reads the titles, URLs, identifiers, window information and last-access timing of open tabs to identify eligible tabs, display the recent-tab list and switch to the selected destination. It displays tab icons from Chrome's local favicon cache. It does not read webpage contents, cookies, passwords or Chrome's saved browsing history. Incognito tabs are excluded.

Chrome may describe Flytab's required Tabs permission as “Read your browsing history.” Flytab uses that permission for open-tab metadata; it does not use Chrome's History API.

## Storage and retention

Flytab temporarily stores tab identifiers and recent-tab order, the switcher's selection and source/window information, temporary session identity and a toolbar-error flag in Chrome's on-device session storage. Flytab does not persist tab titles, URLs or favicon images in that storage, and does not sync this information to another device.

Closed tabs are removed from recent-tab order. Session storage is cleared when Chrome exits or the extension is reloaded, disabled or removed. Chrome suspending the extension's background worker does not clear this session state. You can clear it by disabling or removing Flytab.

## Sharing and tracking

The extension does not sell or share tab information. It has no analytics, advertising, remote crash reporting, user accounts or external network connections. All extension processing is used for tab switching and its interface, including choosing the toolbar icon's light or dark appearance from your device's appearance preference.

## Support and this policy's hosting

If you email support@creativemethod.com, your email address and any information you choose to include will be received through email services and used to respond to your request. This is separate from the extension, which does not automatically send support messages or browsing information. Avoid including sensitive tab titles or URLs in support messages.

The website hosting this policy has its own privacy practices; visiting that website is separate from using Flytab.

## Changes and contact

If Flytab's data practices change, this policy will be updated and its revision date changed. The current policy will be linked from Flytab's Chrome Web Store listing.

For privacy questions or support, contact [support@creativemethod.com](mailto:support@creativemethod.com).
